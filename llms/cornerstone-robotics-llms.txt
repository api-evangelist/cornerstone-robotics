# Cornerstone Robotics

> Cornerstone Robotics (CSR) is a Hong Kong-headquartered surgical robotics manufacturer founded in 2019 at Hong Kong Science Park. It builds the Sentire Endoscopic Surgical System (C1000), a multi-specialty minimally invasive robot-assisted surgery platform developed in-house and indicated for general surgery, gynecology, thoracic and urology. Sentire holds regulatory clearance in China (NMPA), the European Union (CE/MDR) and Singapore (HSA).

## API surface

Cornerstone Robotics publishes **no public API**. As of 2026-08-11 there is no developer portal,
no API reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL specification, no SDK or client library on any public
package registry, no MCP server, no A2A agent card, and no `/.well-known/` discovery document on any
company host. The product is a regulated physical medical device sold through a direct sales and
clinical-service motion, not a software platform. This is an honest zero, not a gap in our research —
see `x-coverage` in `apis.yml` for the probes that establish it.
